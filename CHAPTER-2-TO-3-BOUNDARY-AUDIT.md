## C. Confirmed boundary gaps and ownership

1. **Slayer/Path/black mask:** 2.5 intentionally stops at 51. Train ordinary Slayer to actual 56, verify actual 60 Strength, complete [Path](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Path_of_Glouphrie), then continue to **actual 58** for [Cave Horrors](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Slayer) after Cabin Fever. Path gives a fixed 20,000 Slayer XP but does not automatically guarantee 58 from the 56 floor; read live XP. Black mask is an RNG upgrade, valuable for the *later* long Slayer lane, not a Chapter 2 prerequisite.
2. **Pirate/Morytania:** [Rum Deal → Cabin Fever](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Morytania) belongs with early Chapter 3 Slayer. The Great Brain Robbery follows Cabin Fever, Creature of Fenkenstrain and RFD Pirate Pete, which our route has; complete it as a coherent story block or at least start it before its Harmony Island Hard diary task. [Morytania Hard](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Morytania_Diary) additionally needs the Cave Horror/58 Slayer, a 71 Agility spike-chain check (70 exit may need one level/boost), Kharyrll POH portal/66 Magic, mushroom spore, watermelon seed and completion of lower tiers; Myreque, Tarn, Ghosts Ahoy and Piety are already supplied. Reward: Morytania legs 3, 15k lamp and Robin's daily bone/ecto-token exchange; decide whether this repays the complete diary before imposing it. **Do not** say “finish the rest” as if lower tiers were all done.
3. **Varlamore:** Chapter 1 completes Children of the Sun/Twilight's Promise, but not [Perilous Moons or The Heart of Darkness](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Final_Dawn). [Jagex confirms](https://www.jagex.com/news/witness-the-epic-conclusion-to-old-school-runescape-s-fan-favourite-varlamore-content-with-varlamore-the-final-dawn-available-today) Final Dawn needs both plus **actual 66 Thieving, 52 RC, 52 Fletching**. The latter two non-RC skills clear; 38→52 RC does not. Schedule the two quests as a Varlamore break and Final Dawn once RC52 is live. Its Arkan blade/Prayer XP are outputs, not Chapter 2 carryovers.
4. **WGS:** full direct chain is audited in A. The missing quest is Path. The old ledger's claim that *all* WGS skill gates already clear is false: guaranteed Magic is only 65, while [WGS requires actual 67](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wgs). Check/train the gap in Chapter 3; do not pretend optional combat spell XP solved it.
5. **Other intermediate quest gates:** Source 3-5's [Fallen From Grace](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fallen_From_Grace) has a reported **47 Runecraft** gate, beyond the guaranteed 38; Sailing 62, Crafting 60 and Mining 53 clear. As a recent Sailing release, confirm its journal/ship requirements when writing the page. [Fremennik Exiles](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Fremennik_Exiles) needs actual **55 Runecraft, 60 Slayer and 65 Crafting**; its Fremennik/Mountain Daughter/Heroes quest line and Smithing/Fishing gates clear. [Hopespear's Will](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hopespear%27s_Will) additionally needs **Land of the Goblins**, omitted from our Chapter 2. These belong to Chapter 3, not a 2.7 patch.
6. **Later grandmaster gates:** [MM2 requires actual 69 Slayer and 70 Crafting](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Monkey_Madness_II/Quick_guide) *before starting* the source's maniacal-monkey branch.
