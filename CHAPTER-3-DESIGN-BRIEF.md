# Chapter 3 design brief — graduation, quests and Bowfa-free progression

**Research snapshot: 25 September 2026. Planning document, not player-facing route copy.** Recheck the in-game quest journal, live releases, drop tables and combat calculator when authoring each page. The repository's Chapter 1–2 route is the authority for what this account actually owns; [BRUHsailer's current generated Chapter 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Osrsper/BRUHsailer/main/index.html) is a macro-planning comparator, not a list of guaranteed supplies or a compulsory maxing destination. No Chapter 3 page is authorized by this brief.

## Decision in one paragraph

The mandatory linear guide should graduate at **all quests released at the time the final page is written, a Fire Cape, and a coherent travel/supply/combat foundation**. Those are finite, varied goals with lasting rewards. The player should be capable of learning ordinary solo PvM and an entry raid, but need not own a raid unique or prove mastery. **Bowfa, full crystal, a black mask, Slayer helmet, Ava's assembler, toxic blowpipe, zenytes, first ToA completion and ornate POH are not graduation requirements.** Offer high-value upgrades separately, then move into player-chosen Endgame Paths. This is a decision for the *current* quest set, not a claim that future quests can never change the weapon floor. No 90/95/99 skill is mandatory without a named quest or selected core consumer.

The core can require deliberate combat *competence* without a long unique grind: complete Fight Caves, teach prayer/positioning/supply planning, and equip a reproducible starter arsenal. A rune crossbow (or shop-limb adamant crossbow if the rune limb is dry), enchanted bolts, **green d'hide**, accumulator, Barrows gloves and Eagle Eye are enough to write a plausible Fire Cape and grandmaster-quest route. The crossbow and bolt supply still need an explicit acquisition checkpoint below; they are not Chapter 2 bank guarantees. Some fights improve dramatically with more gear, so the route must include real boss-specific preparation and a training/retry checkpoint, not promise effortless first attempts.

Chapter 1's conservative actual-63 Magic floor is **368,599 XP**. Mandatory Chapter 2 additions are 1,590 High Alchs **103,350**, Lunar Diplomacy **5,000**, Swan Song **15,000**, Horror from the Deep **4,662** (rounding its 4,662.5 down), RFD Lumbridge Guide **2,500**, King's Ransom **5,000**, and Dream Mentor **10,000**: **145,512 post-floor, 514,111 total**. Level 66 is 496,254; level 67 is 547,953. The guaranteed exit is therefore **actual 66+**. Path of Glouphrie adds 5,000, leaving **28,842 XP** to WGS's actual-67 Magic gate at the conservative floor. “Mandatory” means every player must do it even after skipping every optional panel. “Recommended” means a prominent, payoff-led detour with its own stopping condition and a main-route fallback if skipped. “Endgame branch” means a self-selected long/RNG pursuit that can continue after graduation. Future progress UI must store these categories separately; a recommended drop may not add a required checkbox to the quest-cape meter.

## Gauntlet: 2026 mechanics and the real RNG bill

[Jagex's shipped 22 July update](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/summer-sweep-up-gear--pvm-changes?oldschool=1) gives four random demi-boss rooms at cardinal outer positions, reduces demi-boss attack range from 16 to 10, guarantees the first weapon frame from a low-tier NPC, stacks bark/ore/linum, puts shard drops into inventory, narrows shard variance, standardizes ordinary enemy stats, and changes equipment shard prices (T1 weapon 20→0, T2 weapon 60→50; T1 armour 40→50, T2 body 80→100, T2 helm/legs 80→50, T3 armour 80→100). Do not import an earlier proposal as a shipped extra rule. [Jagex's proposal](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/summer-sweep-up-2026?oldschool=1) explicitly said the work was not intended to drastically shorten the Enhanced grind. The current published tables still list **Enhanced 1/400 CG or 1/2,000 regular**, **armour seed 1/50 CG or 1/120 regular** ([Enhanced seed](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Enhanced_crystal_weapon_seed), [armour seed](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Crystal_armour_seed)). A search of official updates and current reward references through **25 September 2026** found no later pity mechanic, new Enhanced source or rate change; recheck this negative finding before publication.

Assuming independent listed rolls, `P(seed by n)=1-(1-1/r)^n`. The complementary column is the chance of still being dry; “expected KC” is **not** a guarantee.

**Time scenarios, not measured post-patch population averages:** regular learner ~12–18 minutes per *successful* completion plus failed attempts, comfortable ~9–12, efficient non-speedrunner ~6–9; CG learner ~15–25 plus failures, comfortable ~10–13, efficient non-speedrunner ~8–10. A comfortable 400 CG successes is ~67–87 hours; 1,000 is ~167–217 hours. Efficient 400 is ~53–67 hours. A comfortable 2,000 regular successes is ~300–400 hours (efficient ~200–300), before early deaths, breaks or gear-making time. The post-July prep relief makes the loop less erratic, but does not shrink the tail of the geometric drop distribution. The player should time their own first 20 successful runs; these ranges are planning envelopes, not promises.

Regular Gauntlet is a good **learner/chill branch**: practise Hunllef, take seed/shard/rune/cash rewards, and enjoy an occasional Enhanced lottery. It is not rational to demand 2,000 regular completions as a mandatory Enhanced route: five times CG's expected rolls and a >54% chance of still being dry at 1,200. The psychologically sound stop is “finish today's few runs, use anything earned, resume the core route”; if Enhanced appears, celebrate and open a Bowfa branch.

Crystal armour is a distinct RNG project. The [full set](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Crystal_armour) boosts an ordinary crystal bow **+30% accuracy/+15% damage** (and Bowfa); body+legs give **+25%/+12.5%**, leaving the head slot for Slayer helm. Body alone gives **+15%/+7.5%**. Full costs six seeds, body+legs five, body three. With independent armour rolls, six seeds average **300 CG** or **720 regular** completions; chance of six by 300/400/600 CG is approximately **55.6%/81.2%/98.1%**, and by 720/1,000 regular approximately **55.5%/83.9%**. This is not a free “easy Bowfa” shortcut. One body at three seeds is a smaller but still random project; body+legs is attractive specifically on Slayer task. The ordinary crystal bow with full set is a real high-defence middle path, but cannot be represented as equivalent to Bowfa or as a mandatory bounded pickup.

The false choice is “Bowfa or Twisted Bow.” Twisted Bow is a Chambers megarare and only a ceiling reference. A **package** of enchanted-crossbow utility, a fast weapon where armour is low, and correct melee/Magic switches covers the core. Do not force Ranged into an encounter whose sensible answer is another style.

The time-to-acquire comparison is decisive for the **mandatory** route. Bowfa may save several minutes per difficult quest fight and many hours over a future large boss log, but Quest Cape contains only a finite number of such kills; the expected ~67–87 comfortable hours for 400 CG successes, plus a 36.7% dry chance *at that point*, cannot plausibly be repaid inside mandatory quest bosses. This is an inference from the model and route volume, not a universal claim that Bowfa is inefficient for a player who later wants thousands of boss/raid kills.

## Fire Cape placement

Place Fight Caves **in early-to-middle Chapter 3**, after the player has a repeatable Ranged ~75–80, 70 Prayer, green d'hide/Barrows gloves/accumulator, sufficient enchanted bolts (diamond for Jad; mixed affordable bolts for waves), ranged potion if live Herblore/bank allows, food and prayer potions. Black d'hide can improve the kit if a piece happens to drop, but is not a prerequisite. Existing 70 Prayer is a strong advantage. Teach 63-wave pacing, safe-spotting, prayer switches, healer tagging and a deliberate Jad rehearsal. The first attempt is a learning run, not a failure of the route. The written default is RCB if obtained, adamant crossbow if dry; a sunlight crossbow or atlatl can help portions *if already owned*. Bowfa and toxic blowpipe are not prerequisites. ## BRUHsailer Step 29 versus this guide's endpoint

The [live source's 3-1–3-29 arc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Osrsper/BRUHsailer/main/index.html) mixes genuinely useful quest prerequisites (WGS/DS2/MM2/DT2, 74 Slayer/BMR, Fire Cape) with a Bowfa+six-armour-seed CG block, large cash/chin/resource assumptions, diary and Combat Achievement sweeps, ship upgrades, and later **Fishing 99, Mining ~97, Ranged ~92, Crafting 93/99, Runecraft 90/95, Construction 83**, high Sailing and related 99 preparation. The existing [boundary audit](CHAPTER-2-TO-3-BOUNDARY-AUDIT.md) decomposes all 29 steps and their assumptions. Some 3-20 quests were already stale as a “cape” guarantee before September's two releases. None of those post-quest 90s/99s is a current Quest Cape gate. A source-efficient 99/resource stockpile is not automatically an Intermediate-guide graduation requirement.

At the proposed endpoint the player has completed the live quest list, major transport areas/spellbooks, Barrows gloves/Piety/defender, a functioning POH/house-tablet loop, Prifddinas, a useful ship, 70 Prayer, Fire Cape and battle experience against Jad plus several grandmaster bosses. **Normal endgame is open, not finished.** GWD, Zulrah, Vorkath, Muspah, Gauntlet, DT2 post-quest bosses, ToA/CoX/ToB and Slayer remain; some are genuinely much more comfortable with additional levels and gear. The only plausible reason to add a *new* mandatory milestone is a concrete foundational system that is inaccessible at that state. Current research found none that requires an unbounded unique. Raids mastery, elite diaries, Masori/Ancestral/Tbow, 99s and collection log are good *post-graduation* goals.

## Coarse Chapter 3 architecture (not page boundaries or copy)

1. **Exit and short catch-ups:** read actual XP, quest journal, bank, rune/bolt/food stack and house travel. Fill Path's 56 Slayer/60 Strength/remaining Magic67; do Path then WGS when boss-ready. Pair Rum Deal→Cabin Fever→Brain Robbery with ordinary Slayer. Offer black mask as a clearly optional, resumable early upgrade.
2. **Quest-chain/context lanes:** Troll Romance→Curse of Arrav; Land of the Goblins; Perilous Moons→Heart; Fallen From Grace; stage RC only to the next live 47→50→52 checks. Apply Exiles and Final Dawn RC rewards before the next 55/60 gap. Use quests, Sailing couriers/charting, Slayer and skill-training sessions as natural context switches.
3. **Fire Cape milestone:** around 75–80+ Ranged, while the cheap arsenal still teaches mechanics; do not postpone to Bowfa. A short Ranged-training method panel should offer AFK/low-attention default and active option, with a live-level stop.
4. **Grandmaster gates:** bring Crafting to 70, Slayer through 69 then 74, Magic to 75, Runecraft to 60, Woodcutting74, Smithing/Cooking72, Fletching70 only as their consumers arrive. Do MM2, DS2, Secrets/BCS/DT2, Final Dawn, Night at the Theatre/BMR with boss-specific preflights and style switches. WGS/Exiles/Final Dawn rewards can significantly reduce subsequent gaps.
5. **Current-quest reconciliation and graduation:** short independent quests including September releases, claim lamps, check *every* live journal entry, finish remaining mandatory miniquest dependencies, then verify cape/Fire Cape/travel/supplies and explain Endgame Paths. Do not assume a source-guide quest subset is the entire live list.
6. **Parallel optional Bowfa branch, never a dependency arrow into step 4:** learn regular Gauntlet; try CG at comfortable stats; choose 5–20 run session goals, record seed/shard progress, return to quests/Slayer/Sailing when bored. Use an armour/body seed immediately if practical. If Enhanced drops, craft Bowfa after its 80 Ranged/70 Agility check and continue toward desired armour; if it does not, graduate anyway. No single “leave when Enhanced drops” checkbox and no fixed 70-hour/50m promise.
