Targeted correction pass starts from approved commit `576c5bf2108e1b809309bf1c536b198cc653f736` on the same date. This report covers the 17 Intermediate pages (1.1–1.4, 2.1–2.7, 3.1–3.6), 284 numbered route/method blocks, the overview, progress components, existing chapter ledgers and prior audits. It does **not** claim an in-game playthrough. The companion [resource ledger](RESOURCE-ACQUISITION-LEDGER.csv) now records **93 allocations** with first consumer, form, supplier, inputs, reserve, recovery, BRUHsailer comparison and mechanics link. It is an allocation ledger, not a claim that there are only 93 named items in 284 blocks: mundane one-off dialogue items remain in the linked quest phase guides unless their supply or reservation was material to this audit.

## Comparator and method

The linked [BRUHsailer guide](https://umkyzn.github.io/BRUHsailer/) is dynamically rendered. The complete underlying [source repository](https://github.com/umkyzn/BRUHsailer) was cloned and read at commit `6422739188591a5ae18f41a424d7b1d29835f543`; `data/guide_data.json` says `updatedOn: 2026-08-30`. Retrieval and current-mechanics research were on 15 October 2026. All three source chapters, nine sections and 226 route steps—including nested instructions, item lists and background notes—were compared in chapter ranges with the local 17 pages. No source section was inaccessible. The repository remains authoritative for *our* mandatory/optional route; current [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/) quest, item, shop, Farming and minigame references resolve mechanics, not BRUHsailer's older or larger target. Where web extraction was blocked, search excerpts were cross-checked against the source page named in the ledger; no game client was available to verify live inventory or shop interfaces.

The backward pass asked what supplies each mandatory consumer really has **after earlier uses**. The forward pass asked which outputs of retained or removed methods are actually needed. Classifications in the CSV distinguish a fixed quest reward from a repeatable shop/spawn, a variable drop/harvest with recovery, a time gate and an optional source. An accessible random drop is not labeled deterministic. Cash in kingdom coffers, unalched items and gross alchemy receipts are not liquid coins.

## Remaining uncertainty and scope limits

- **Random requests and yields are not guarantees.** Fairytale I chooses three actual quest items; its step now directs a player to read the roll and the current item-specific source table. Forgettable Tale's Rowdy dwarf can ask for a different item. Gem bags, herb drops, belladonna/cadantine seeds, limpwurt harvests and Gorak key halves have no fixed completion time. The ledger calls these variable with recovery, not deterministic. The guide does not fully reproduce every Fairytale I randomized item branch; this is the main remaining *in-guide* acquisition-detail limit, though the linked current table supplies each branch.
- **Cash and retry inventory are account-dependent.** No numeric lower bound for Chapter 3 liquid coins or lifetime Prayer/antifire consumption follows from this route. Prior 35m Sepulchre arithmetic and gross battlestaff/CG proceeds cannot be credited. Before each paid/reclaim encounter, the live bank and currency must be audited. A long poor-drop streak increases time, not a required rare-weapon branch.
- **Complete source review is not game execution.** All local page text and BRUHsailer data were readable, and current mechanics references were consulted for disputed suppliers. No account was played through, no live bank was observed, and no Hardcore death rule was personally tested. A successful Astro build validates rendering, not shop stock or RNG in game.
- **No route redesign proposed now.** If the early 107-piece gold-jewelry target proves disproportionate in live play, a future design review could reduce convenience quantities and rely on shorter replenishment batches. This audit does not silently reorder Shilo Village or import BRUHsailer's high-input resource grind.
