## 77 Thieving and liquidity: quantitative macro review (not implemented)

The exact level-50 floor entering Rogue's Den/Wealthy Citizens is **101,333 XP**. Because the route explicitly bridges to 50, earlier quest XP is included in that live checkpoint and must not be added again. Level 72 is **899,257 XP** and level 77 is **1,475,581 XP**: 50→72 costs **797,924 XP**; 50→77 costs **1,374,248 XP**; the 72→77 tail is **576,324 XP**. Later mandatory gates do not require 77: While Guthix Sleeps requires **actual 72 Thieving**, The Curse of Arrav requires 62, and the optional Dorgesh-Kaan rich-chest diary action is separately checked at 78 if retained. Later guaranteed rewards include **80,000 Thieving XP from While Guthix Sleeps**, **55,000 from The Final Dawn**, and **40,000 from The Curse of Arrav**—at least **175,000 XP** by 3.3, before smaller quest rewards and a later **50,000 from Dragon Slayer II** if completed. These arrive *after* their gates and do not make 77 itself a requirement.

With full Rogue kept, wealthy-citizen pickpocketing yields more house keys; **burgled valuables are not doubled**. The current activity reference gives about **72k Thieving XP/h at level 50, 80k at 60, 93k at 70**, rising toward 95k at 80. It quotes **90–125k GP/h from valuables during house-burgling time**; the associated money guide budgets about **one hour of key pickpocketing per 3.5 hours of houses**. Weighting that split and adding the ordinary pickpocket coins gives a rough **90–120k liquid GP/h over the whole loop**, excluding jewellery that might be banked rather than sold/alched. Thus 50→77 is roughly **15–19 hours** of this relaxed method and often produces on the order of **1.35–2.3m liquid GP**; the 1.35m target is usually reached by or before 77 but must be checked, not guaranteed by the level. Level 50→72 is roughly **9–11 hours**, usually about **0.8–1.3m GP**; if cash is short, continuing the *same* activity to 1.35m also advances Thieving. For a coupled illustration rather than a promise, at 82k XP/h and 105k GP/h, the current 77 stop takes about **16.8h** and earns **1.76m**; the 72 stop takes **9.7h**, then about **3.1h** more cash work reaches 1.35m and leaves Thieving around level 73. Actual rates and bank loot vary.

**Recommendation for a separate approved change:** retain full Rogue, but consider replacing the fixed 77 checkbox with **actual 72 plus the same 1.35m liquid-cash checkpoint**, then stop. This likely removes several hours of extra early Thieving without pushing the same XP into a later *required* grind, because no identified quest consumes 77 and the named later quests award at least 175k regardless. It does sacrifice some early excess GP/Thieving and should be reassessed against a live cash ledger. **This pass leaves 77 and Rogue required.**
