## Resource, cash, optional-dependency and boundary checks

- **Reserved items:** 16 magic logs have explicit later quest uses (Desert Treasure, Mourning's End I, Monkey Madness II balloon); keep them distinct from birdhouse logs. The 1,600 glass is now 1,590 orbs + 6 Desert Treasure + 1 Hand in the Sand + 1 clockwork-table glass + 2 spare. Additional Crafting training uses separately obtained glass/gems. Pads, jewellery and quest-item reserves are checked at consumption rather than assumed from random Wintertodt/birdhouse loot.
- **Runes:** MTA's nature/law/cosmic costs are paid from its opening stock once. The 2.1 alchemy nature balance is rechecked, and 1.4 buys 4,900 cosmics for 4,770 orb casts plus a modest buffer. The 100-chaos Chapter 1 reserve is protected. The optional cat loop cannot be counted as a guaranteed death-rune bank for Chapter 3.
- **Cash:** 1.35m is the 1.2 departure target. At *flat initial shop prices* alone, 1,650 natures (~297k), 700 laws (~168k) and 400 cosmics (~20k) approach ~485k before water/mind/elementals, Magic training, sand (~80k) and other purchases; stock depletion and world choice change real spend. Another 4,900 cosmics are ~245k at initial 50 GP, before price drift. The 1,590-staff buy/craft/alch cycle is self-financing in **batches**; 9,300 GP per High Alch is ~14.79m gross sales across the whole stock, *not* ~14.79m profit or an end-of-chapter cash balance. At a roughly 7,000-GP staff price, the raw staff→alch spread is ~2,300 each / ~3.66m total **before** nature runes, transport and other costs. Planks (~100–113k oak and ~450–500k teak at suggested stocks), lectern gold leaf, Iban upgrade, Barrows gloves, kingdom funding/draw, Sailing hull and quest supplies consume further cash. Giants' Foundry can add variable income, not a fixed floor. The route now explicitly refills at the **first pre-orb cash pinch**; later purchases remain live-bank/batch decisions. Do not count deposited kingdom money or unalched alchables as liquid coins.

## Major proposals held for separate macro review

1. **Angler outfit:** present route spends a potentially long random Trawler session for a 2.5% Fishing-XP benefit without a named gate. Compare full-outfit time with just continuing normal Fishing/Agility and make it optional if the latter wins.
2. **Early Bones to Peaches:** front-loading full MTA provides a useful lifetime unlock but presses the early Magic/rune/cash budget. A deferral requires recalculating Magic XP and could move multiple checkpoints; keep current route until that entire chain is redesigned deliberately.
3. **Rogue outfit / 77 Thieving / 1.35m:** the cash-generating activity and the level are not interchangeable. Test how much Rogue doubles *actual pickpocket-derived keys/loot* during this route, then compare a smaller Thieving stop plus task-sized cash refills. Do not delete the reliable current opening cash reserve by assertion.
4. **~74 Fishing:** its combined Agility and food output is real, but 74 is above known hard Fishing gates. Quantify the replacement Agility/food work before lowering it.
