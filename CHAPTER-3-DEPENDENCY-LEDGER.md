# Chapter 3 dependency ledger

## Frozen Chapter 3 doctrine

- Core graduation means the current Quest Cape, Fire Cape and reliable account infrastructure, followed by player-directed endgame choices.
- No 90/95/99 target is mandatory merely because BRUHsailer trains it.
- Bowfa/Corrupted Gauntlet is an optional, resumable Endgame Path. Optional RNG drops never feed mandatory progress.
- Claim quest rewards before later skill grinds whenever dependencies permit; preserve a sensible context-switching rhythm.

## 3.1 decisions

- Run the pirate chain before Slayer training. The Great Brain Robbery's selectable 5,000-XP lamp goes to Slayer.
- Read live XP and train only the remainder to actual 56 Slayer; check actual 60 Strength before The Path of Glouphrie.
- Complete Path at its first valid opportunity and claim its fixed 20,000 Slayer, 30,000 Strength, 5,000 Magic and 5,000 Thieving XP before further training.
- Then read live XP, finish only to actual 58 Slayer, and offer black mask as an optional/resumable branch.
- Then read live Magic XP and bridge only to actual 67 Magic. WGS is the page finale, with no pre-quest combat mega-grind.
- WGS unlocks normal Tormented Demons. A tormented synapse or demonbane weapon remains an optional future gear branch, not a 3.1 exit condition.

## 3.2 decisions

- Perilous Moons comes before Slayer 60 and the first Runecraft training. Its fixed 40,000 Slayer and 5,000 Runecraft XP leave, at the exact 58-Slayer floor, 9,276 XP to Slayer 60 before any other rewards. Claim its normal Lunar Chest reward once; Moon uniques are optional.
- The Heart of Darkness follows while in Varlamore and is required before The Final Dawn. Its verified 8,000 Slayer XP reduces the exact-floor Slayer-60 remainder to **1,276 XP** before Duradel's notes or incidental tasks. Train only that live remainder to actual 60 for The Fremennik Exiles; not 69.
- Read Runecraft XP after each quest, then train to only the next hard gate: **47 → Fallen From Grace (+5,000 RC, +10,000 Crafting) → 50 → Devious Minds (+5,000 RC) → 52 → The Final Dawn (+25,000 RC) → 55 → The Fremennik Exiles (+30,000 RC)**. With Perilous Moons' 5,000 RC, this is 70,000 fixed RC XP across 3.2. Never count reward XP against the quest's own entry gate. No RC 60 yet.
- Fallen From Grace requires the player's own **raft or skiff**, not a sloop; use the retained camphor-hulled skiff. Pack supplies before Wyrmscraig: teleport access arrives only after the quest, and its bank chest is unavailable during the quest (and requires construction afterward if desired). Mad Angel repeats, Hallowfell, Golem Crafting and Mortimer tasks are optional.
- Apply Fallen's 10,000 Crafting XP before training only to actual **65 Crafting**. The Fremennik Exiles consumes 65 and rewards 50,000 more; defer 70 until its later consumer. The route's actual 67+ Magic does not guarantee the 77-Magic Superglass Make spell.
- A non-degraded large/colossal essence pouch is mandatory for Devious Minds. Check bank and GOTR rewards; if absent, obtain small → medium → large through eligible Abyssal-creature rolls in the ALR Abyssal Area or GOTR. The August 2026 rates are **up to** 1/5 for walkers and 1/8 for guardians, not deterministic kill counts. The quest consumes an ordinary large pouch but not a colossal; its Law Altar phase still crosses low-level Wilderness through the Abyss.
- The Final Dawn requires both earlier Varlamore quests, actual 52 Runecraft and its other covered gates. Ennius is melee-only due to Ranged/Magic protection; Ranged is useful for Metzli. The Arkan blade is a situational reward, not a universal upgrade or prerequisite for the next page.
- Allocate The Final Dawn's **one** 55,000-combat-XP lamp to **Ranged by default**. At the exact actual-60 Ranged floor, 273,742 + 55,000 = 328,742 XP, just **5,062 XP** short of actual 62 (333,804 XP). If optional play already left Ranged comfortably Fire-Cape-ready, put this same lamp into Magic for its later actual-75 quest gate instead. Never count or use it twice.
- Exiles consumes actual 60 Slayer, 65 Crafting and 55 Runecraft, then awards 50,000 / 50,000 / 30,000 XP respectively. At exact entry floors its postquest floors are Slayer 323,742 XP (61+), Crafting 499,428 XP (66+), Runecraft 196,636 XP (56+). A Fremennik shield is mandatory but can be obtained deterministically from Bardur after exile (pet rock, Ranging Guild rune thrownaxe special attack, ring of charos(a) or 150,000 coins); the similarly named Neitiznot shield is not the item. Buy two kegs of beer before starting. Record live XP. Basilisk jaw/faceguard farming is optional. No Slayer 69, Crafting 70 or Runecraft 60 grind here.
- The Fremennik Exiles' hard skill gates are **65 Crafting, 60 Slayer, 60 Smithing, 60 Fishing and 55 Runecraft**. **60 Mining is not a hard gate**: it is a boostable check only on the choice to mine three lunar ores instead of bringing three lunar bars.

## 3.3 decisions

- After the single Final Dawn lamp, train only the live remainder to **actual 62 Ranged** for The Curse of Arrav. This is a quest gate, **not** a Fight Cave readiness target. The cheap default is Nardok's Dorgeshuun crossbow and bone bolts at Gemstone/Ammonite crabs, with suitable Slayer tasks as a change of scene.
- Complete **Troll Romance → The Curse of Arrav** before the long Ranged grind. Arrav consumes actual 62 Ranged plus the previously cleared 64 Mining, 62 Thieving, 61 Agility, 58 Strength, 37 Slayer and Defender of Varrock gates. Its 40,000-XP Mining/Thieving/Agility rewards land; Armoured Zombie axe/helmet drops remain optional.
- **Bank-audit usable ammo before any production training.** The one required progress outcome is a deterministic Fight Cave crossbow and sufficient ammo, not separate 73-Smithing/63-Fletching/65-Fletching checkboxes. The **adamant crossbow** is the guaranteed weapon floor from Keldagrim-stocked limbs, mahogany stock and crossbow string at already-cleared 61 Fletching. Use an RCB if already banked, but never farm runite limbs or an RCB drop for mandatory progress. Broad bolts need RCB or better. Pinned comparisons put adamant crossbow only roughly 4–4.6% behind RCB on Jad, Galvek, quest Vorkath and quest Leviathan.
- Planning buffer: about **1,200 plain adamant bolts** for Fight Cave waves/retry headroom, **250 diamond (e)** for Jad/high-defence and later quests, and **300 ruby (e)** mainly saved for later high-HP bosses. These are bank-adjusted buffers, not exact consumption predictions. Only if stock is short, actual **73 Smithing** makes adamant bolt bases and actual **63–65 Fletching** makes ruby/diamond tips; neither is a universal Chapter 3.3 or Quest Cape gate (the later quest Smithing floor is 72). The zero-stock example uses 175 adamant bars, 1,750 feathers, about 25 rubies and 21 diamonds.
- Split training **62→70 Ranged → A Ruff Situation and Crab Quest → 70→75 Ranged**. The two September 2026 quests are current Quest Cape requirements and a light break, not Jad upgrades. **75 Ranged** is the normal first-Fire-Cape target, not a hard Fight Cave gate. Experienced players may attempt around 70; 75→80 is a bounded retry comfort adjustment.
- The mandatory/default Jad plan is **Ranged only**. Stay outside melee range to reduce his prayer recognition to **Protect from Missiles vs Protect from Magic**. Switch/confirm prayer, do only one secondary action, then watch Jad again. Tag healers one at a time and use Longrange if a click might path into Jad's melee reach. The Fight Caves is safe for ordinary and solo Hardcore Ironmen, unsafe for Hardcore Group Ironman lives; the August 2026 re-entry cooldown removal allows immediate retry.
- The **Fire Cape is guaranteed at the 3.3 exit** and must be assumed by later melee/combat pages. Bowfa, crystal, blowpipe, Rigour, RCB RNG, black mask/Slayer helmet and Ava's assembler remain outside mandatory progress. Do not infer a 3.4 route from this ledger.
